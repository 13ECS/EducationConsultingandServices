Present Simple Positive with other verbs

  • (he / go to school every day) (He goes to school every day.)
  • (I / like swimming) (I like swimming.)
  • (you / play badminton on Saturdays) (You play badminton on Saturdays.)
  • (the class / begin at 9 am) (The class begins at 9am.)
  • (they / sometimes go to the cinema) (They sometimes go to the cinema.)
  • (she / love chocolate) (She loves chocolate.)
  • (we / study French) (We study French.)
  • (they / live in London) (They live in London.)
  • (he / work in a restaurant) (He works in a restaurant.)
  • (Lucy / play the guitar) (Lucy plays the guitar.)

  • (he / clean the house at the weekends) (He cleans the house at the weekends.)
