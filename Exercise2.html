Present Simple Other Verbs Positive Exercise 2

Make Positive Present Simple Sentences

  • (we / often watch TV) (We often watch TV.)
  • (I / hate mushrooms) (I hate mushrooms.)
  • (she / visit her grandmother every Christmas) (She visits her grandmother every Christmas.)
  • (they / use the Internet every evening) (They use the Internet every evening.)
  • (you / often go swimming) (You often go swimming.)
  • (John / hate waiting for the bus) (John hates waiting for the bus.)
  • (Lucy and Jill / want to go out tonight) (Lucy and Jill want to go out tonight.)
  • (I / usually stay at home on Fridays) (I usually stay at home on Fridays.)
  • (he / love driving fast cars) (He loves driving fast cars.)
  • (they / often have parties) (They often have parties.)
  • (she / take a piano lesson every Monday) (She takes a piano lesson every Monday.)
  • (we / like watching French films) (We like watching French films.)
  • (you / go to bed very early) (You go to bed very early.)
  • (I / always eat breakfast) (I always eat breakfast.)
  • (they / often arrive late) (They often arrive late.)
  • (he / live in Beijing) (He lives in Beijing.)
  • (David / work in a school) (David works in a school.)
  • (she / read a lot of novels) (She reads a lot of novels.)
  • (the Queen / like her dogs) (The Queen likes her dogs.)
  • (I / cook almost every night) (I cook almost every night.)
